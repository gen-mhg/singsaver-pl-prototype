Frequently asked questions
How do personal loans work?

A personal loan provides you with a lump sum of money that you repay with interest over a set period.

How long does it take to get a personal loan?

Approval and disbursement timelines vary by lender, with some offering same-day or next-day processing while others require several days.

Can foreigners get a loan in Singapore?

Yes, foreigners can apply for personal loans in Singapore, but they may have to meet additional eligibility criteria.

What bank is easiest to get a personal loan from?

Different banks have varying requirements; generally S$20,000–30,000 minimum annual income is standard for citizens and PRs.

What is the lending rate in Singapore?

Average lending rates for personal loans can range from 2.5% to 10% p.a., but these are subject to individual assessment.

How does a personal loan affect your credit score?

Making timely repayments can improve your credit score, while late payments or defaulting on the loan can negatively impact it.

How much would a S$5,000 loan cost per month?

Monthly costs depend on interest rate and repayment period; use the loan calculator for accurate estimates.

Which bank has the lowest interest rate for personal loans?

Personal loan interest rates in Singapore vary depending on the bank, your income, credit score, and loan tenure.
