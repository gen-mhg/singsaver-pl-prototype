Interest rate column — Strapi config → product table

A fixed, purpose-built Interest rate column for the SG PL comparison table. It stacks two existing attributes in one cell — Annual Interest Rate (top) and EIR (bottom) — to free a column slot, without merging the data. The two attributes and the labels are hardcoded — there is no attribute picker and no editable labels. It's additive: the separate AIR and EIR columns stay available. The right shows the live product table; both AIR and EIR remain independently sortable via Sort by.
